A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Do I need an account?+

No. Choose local setup and start tracking right away. Google sign-in is optional and used for backup.

What changed in version 1.3.0?+

Settings now has compact tracker cards, progress feedback, and background backup syncing. Typography controls apply fonts consistently and save rapid changes in order. Privacy and support links are included.

Will it remind me when the app is closed?+

With reminders enabled, closing the window keeps TrackVerse running in the system tray. Choosing Quit stops reminders. Open the app again after restarting Windows; automatic startup is not enabled.

Why might Windows block the download?+

The current installer and app are unsigned. A free signing application is under review. A clean malware scan does not guarantee Smart App Control acceptance. Keep Windows protections enabled and see the code signing policy for status.

ABOUT TRACKVERSE

A habit tracker by Vaibhav Pandey.

Vaibhav Pandey creates and maintains TrackVerse, a free, open-source habit tracker for Windows. Track daily habits, set targets and reminders, review your progress, and make time for focused work with the built-in Pomodoro timer.

Your routine starts offline, with local storage and no account required. Optional Google backup helps you keep a copy of your tracker in your own Drive.
