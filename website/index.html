Local first, with a backup

Start locally with no account. Or sign in with Google to create private tracker spreadsheets in your own Drive, sync when online, and recover completed backups after reinstalling.

GOOGLE CONNECTION

Only the files TrackVerse uses.

Google sign-in is optional. TrackVerse uses your basic account information to identify your connected account, and per-file Drive access to create and update its tracker spreadsheets. It does not request access to all your Drive files.

Create separate trackers for everyday habits, exam preparation, or a new routine. Each cloud tracker has its own local SQLite data and Google spreadsheet.
