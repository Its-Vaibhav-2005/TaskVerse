## 00:08 — Meet TrackVerse

TrackVerse brings your habits, focus time, and progress into one calm workspace. Built for Windows, with your everyday work saved locally.

## 00:18 — The problem

When routines are scattered, it is hard to see what needs attention. A monthly habit grid gives every small action a place.

## 00:28 — The workspace

Plan your month, check in today, and review your consistency. Then use a habit-linked timer to make space for focused work.

## 00:38 — 01 · Choose local setup

Start at the welcome screen. Choose Set up locally to use the native SQLite database without an account. Google backup is optional, and can be connected later.

## 00:51 — 02 · Create a habit

In Overview, select New habit. Begin with something specific and achievable, such as drinking water or reading twenty pages.

## 01:02 — 03 · Schedule and personalize

Give the habit a name and category. Choose the weekdays you want to repeat, then select a color and icon. Add it to this month, or all existing months.

## 01:14 — 04 · Daily targets and reminders

For repeated actions, set a daily target. Here, water has a target of eight. Turn on Remind me, choose the first reminder, and set how often it repeats.

## 01:28 — Reminder behavior

On Windows, reminders repeat on scheduled days until the target is complete or the day ends. Keep TrackVerse running, including in the tray. Windows notification settings still apply.

## 01:42 — 05 · Save your habit

Select Create habit. Your habit appears in the month grid, with today highlighted. Only today’s scheduled cells can be changed; past and future dates stay protected.

## 01:55 — 06 · Record partial progress

Click today’s cell to record progress. For a target-based habit, use plus or minus, then Save progress. Partial counts are saved, so you can return later.

## 02:08 — 07 · Reach the target

When the count reaches the target, save again to complete the day. Single-target habits need just one click. The dashboard updates automatically.

## 02:20 — 08 · Find and filter

Use Find a habit to search by name. The category filter narrows the grid to areas such as learning, fitness, or work.

## 02:30 — 09 · Edit and manage

Open a habit’s three-dot menu to edit its name, schedule, target, or reminders. Deletion asks for confirmation and removes that habit’s check-ins across its assigned months.

## 02:43 — 10 · Plan the next month

Use the plus on the month bar to plan ahead. Choose the month and year, then select Add month.

## 02:51 — 11 · Reuse or start fresh

Reuse the previous month’s habits, or start fresh. Each month has its own membership, while shared habits keep their identity. Only future month tabs can be removed.

## 03:05 — 12 · Read your dashboard

At the top, see completion rate, total check-ins, your best current streak, and active habits. These are real app calculations, using demonstration history in this video.

## 03:18 — 13 · Weekly and daily insights

Weekly summaries reveal your rhythm. The consistency curve shows daily completion, and Today at a glance shows today’s remaining habits, even when another month is selected.

## 03:30 — How progress is calculated

Completion rate uses scheduled opportunities through today. Future days and dates before a habit was created are excluded. Streaks follow scheduled days; an unfinished today breaks a streak only when the day ends.

## 03:45 — 14 · Start a focus session

Open Pomodoro from the sidebar. Choose the habit you want to focus on, then set work and break durations. Press play when you are ready.

## 03:57 — 15 · Pause, resume, finish

Pause whenever you need to, and resume when you return. Finish and reset saves elapsed work. Breaks and paused time are excluded from your focus total.

## 04:09 — 16 · Floating timer

Switch to mini view for a compact, resizable timer that stays above ordinary windows. Closing the widget hides it; timing continues while the main application is running.

## 04:22 — 17 · Focus Insights

Open Insights to see work time divided by habit. The colors match your habits. Focus time and daily completion are separate, so using the timer does not automatically check in a habit.

## 04:36 — 18 · Choose your appearance

In Settings, choose System to follow Windows appearance, or select Light or Dark. The selected palette carries through the workspace, dialogs, charts, and timer.

## 04:48 — 19 · Explore 43 themes

Browse forty-three theme presets, each with light and dark palettes. Search by name or collection, inspect the previews, and select the atmosphere that works for you.

## 05:00 — 20 · Typography

Typography offers Inter, Lora, and JetBrains Mono, plus one-point text-size adjustments. A live preview helps you choose, and Reset typography restores the defaults. The bundled fonts work offline.

## 05:15 — 21 · Portable backups

Export backup saves habits, month tabs, check-ins, and focus sessions as JSON. Restore backup loads a saved copy after confirmation and replaces the active tracker’s data. Export first before replacing a plan.

## 05:32 — 22 · Optional Google backup

For optional cloud backup, expand Trackers and cloud backup, then choose Continue with Google. Sign-in creates or finds private backup spreadsheets automatically. This step requires your own Google account.

## 05:47 — Connected tracker tools

After connecting, named trackers let you separate different plans. You can create, find, rename, switch, sync, or open their sheets. Recovery depends on finished syncs. Deleting a cloud tracker moves its sheet to Drive’s Trash.

## 06:05 — Local storage and recovery

The Windows app saves everyday work in SQLite, including offline. You can disconnect Google without deleting local habits. Local databases and exported backups are not encrypted, so store your copies thoughtfully.
