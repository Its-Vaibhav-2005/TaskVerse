# TrackVerse — cinematic demo & tutorial

Voice: Andrew, neural English (US).

## 00:00:00 — MEET TRACKVERSE

Big changes start with small steps. Meet TrackVerse. Make room for better habits, and see your progress take shape.

## 00:00:09 — YOUR DAILY RITUALS

When routines live in scattered notes, it is easy to lose the thread. TrackVerse brings your habits, monthly plans, and focus time into one workspace.

## 00:00:22 — 01 / GET STARTED

On the desktop welcome screen, choose Set up locally to begin without an account. Your data is saved on this device in SQLite. Google sign in is available for optional backup.

## 00:00:35 — 02 / CREATE A HABIT

Start with one achievable action. In the habit tracker, choose New habit. Give it a specific name, such as Read twenty pages.

## 00:00:45 — 02 / MAKE IT YOURS

Choose a category, select the weekdays you want to repeat, and pick a color and icon. Choose this month only, or all existing months. Then select Create habit.

## 00:00:57 — 03 / CHECK IN TODAY

Find your habit row and today’s highlighted column. Click the scheduled cell to mark it complete. The check appears, and your progress updates automatically.

## 00:01:08 — A ROUTINE THAT STAYS HONEST

You can click again today to undo. Past and future dates stay locked, and only scheduled days can be checked. Focus on the action you can take today.

## 00:01:19 — 04 / PLAN THE NEXT MONTH

To plan ahead, select the plus beside your month tabs. Choose the month and year, then Add month. You can reuse your habits or start a fresh monthly plan.

## 00:01:31 — 05 / UNDERSTAND YOUR PROGRESS

The dashboard shows completion rate, total check ins, your best current streak, and active habits. Here, one scheduled action is complete. The numbers reflect this demonstration, not a promised result.

## 00:01:46 — 06 / MAKE TIME TO FOCUS

For focused work, open Pomodoro under Widgets in the desktop sidebar. Choose the habit you want to work on, then set your work and break durations.

## 00:01:57 — 06 / START YOUR SESSION

Press Start to begin. Pause when you need to, and resume when you are ready. Finish or reset to save elapsed work. Breaks and paused time are excluded. The timer does not check off the habit for you.

## 00:02:11 — KEEP FOCUS IN VIEW

Switch to the compact view to keep the floating timer above your other windows. Recorded work time appears in Focus Insights, grouped by habit, so you can see where your time went.

## 00:02:24 — 07 / FIND YOUR ATMOSPHERE

In Settings, open Appearance and browse forty three themes. Every preset supports light and dark modes. Search for a palette, then choose the atmosphere that helps you settle in.

## 00:02:37 — 07 / MAKE IT COMFORTABLE

Follow your system appearance, or choose light or dark manually. Typography controls let you choose a bundled font and adjust the overall text size for comfortable reading.

## 00:02:50 — 08 / KEEP A COPY

In Data and privacy, choose Export backup to keep a portable JSON copy. Restore backup lets you bring a saved copy back. Optional Google Sheets backup is managed separately under Trackers and cloud backup.

## 00:03:05 — SMALL ACTIONS. VISIBLE PROGRESS.

A clear plan. A daily check in. A little time for focused work. TrackVerse brings those small actions together, so you can understand your routine and keep building it.
