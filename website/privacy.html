YOUR DATA, EXPLAINED

Privacy policy

Effective October 22, 2026 · Applies to the TrackVerse Windows application

Who operates TrackVerse

TrackVerse is a habit-tracking application maintained by Vaibhav Pandey. Contact vaibhavpandey.cs31@gmail.com for privacy questions or support. This policy describes the current Windows application.

Local-only use

You can choose “Set up locally” without creating an account. Your habit names, categories, schedules, colors, icons, creation dates, completion dates, month tabs, Pomodoro focus-session habit identifiers, names, start times and durations, and application preferences are stored in SQLite databases on your device. Local-only trackers are not uploaded by the app. Exported backups are saved where you choose.

Optional Google sign-in

If you choose “Continue with Google,” Google handles authentication in your browser. TrackVerse receives your Google account identifier, name, and email address to display and associate the connected account with its trackers. TrackVerse does not receive your Google password.

TrackVerse requests basic identity scopes (openid, email, and profile) and the Google Drive per-file scope (drive.file). This allows the app to create tracker spreadsheets and read or update files it is authorized to use. The app does not request unrestricted access to your Google Drive, Gmail, contacts, or calendar.

What is backed up

Cloud trackers upload habit records, completion dates, month tabs, focus-session history, tracker aliases, backup timestamps, and version information to spreadsheets in your Google account. The app creates a readable summary and a versioned backup history. Backup history is compressed and encoded, not end-to-end encrypted. Anyone you give access to the spreadsheet may be able to read or reconstruct that data.

Spreadsheets are created without public sharing. TrackVerse does not require “Anyone with the link” access. Your Google account's sharing settings and any applicable organization rules control access.

Where data goes

The Windows app communicates directly with Google authentication, Drive, and Sheets services for sign-in and synchronization. It does not upload habit data to a separate TrackVerse server. The current app has no advertising or analytics SDK and does not sell habit data or use it for advertising. Google processes data under its own privacy policy.

If you contact support by email, information you send is received by the maintainer and the email services involved. Avoid sending passwords, OAuth files, or tokens.

Credentials and device security

Google access and refresh tokens are saved locally using Electron's safeStorage encryption on Windows. Tokens authorize the app to continue syncing without repeated sign-in. Local SQLite habit databases and exported JSON backups are not encrypted by TrackVerse; protect your device and backup files accordingly.

Your choices and deletion

You can use local-only setup, switch trackers, export a backup, or disconnect Google in Settings. Disconnecting removes the app's saved Google tokens from the device; it does not delete local habits or Google spreadsheets. You can revoke TrackVerse's access from your Google account connections.

To delete cloud records, first disconnect or revoke access to prevent further synchronization, then delete the associated TrackVerse spreadsheets from Google Drive and empty their trash if you want permanent deletion. Version history lives in those spreadsheets. To remove local records, remove the application's data directory and any exported backups you have saved. Uninstalling the preview alone may preserve application data.

Backup limits

Offline changes remain on your device until a backup succeeds. Only successfully uploaded data can be recovered from Google after losing local files. Google outages, quota limits, revoked permissions, manual spreadsheet edits, or deletion can interrupt synchronization. Keep a separate exported backup for important records.

Changes to this policy

This page will be updated when the application's data practices change. The effective date above identifies this version.
